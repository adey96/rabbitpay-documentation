RabbitPay allows customers to pay through a **UPI QR Code directly inside the checkout popup**.

Once your Razorpay QR Code is created, RabbitPay automatically displays it during checkout so customers can scan and pay without being redirected to a separate payment page.

## Step 1: Create Your Razorpay QR Code

If you already have a Razorpay QR Code, you can skip this step.

If you don't have one yet, create it first using the guide below.

<Card title="Don't have a Razorpay QR Code?" icon="qrcode">
  Create your QR Code in Razorpay before continuing with this setup.

  [**Generate Razorpay QR Code →**](https://docs.rabbitpay.ai/integrations/razorpay/generate-qr-code)
</Card>

<Note>
  The QR Code only needs to be created once. After it is created in Razorpay, RabbitPay can use it for UPI QR payments at checkout.
</Note>

---

## Step 2: Open RabbitPay Checkout

After your Razorpay QR Code is created, open your Shopify store and launch **RabbitPay Checkout**.

Customers will see **Pay via UPI** along with the other available payment methods.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 11 193830](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-11-193830.png "Screenshot 2026 08 11 193830")
</Frame>

---

## Step 3: Scan and Pay

When the customer selects **Pay via UPI and click on Show QR**, RabbitPay displays the Razorpay QR Code directly inside the checkout popup.

The customer can:

1. Scan the QR Code using their UPI app.
2. Confirm the payment.
3. Complete the order without leaving RabbitPay Checkout.

<Frame caption="UPI QR Code displayed directly inside RabbitPay Checkout.">
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 11 193043 1](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-11-193043-1.png "Screenshot 2026 08 11 193043 1")
</Frame>

Customers can use supported UPI apps such as **Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and CRED** to scan and complete the payment.

## Result

Your Razorpay QR Code is now available directly inside **RabbitPay Checkout**.

Customers can select **Pay via UPI**, scan the QR Code, and complete their payment without being redirected to a separate payment page.
