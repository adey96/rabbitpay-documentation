# How to Verify Shopify COD Orders Using OTP

OTP Verification helps verify genuine customers before they proceed to checkout. RabbitPay sends a One-Time Password (OTP) via **SMS** or **WhatsApp**, ensuring that only verified customers can continue with their order.

---

## Why Use OTP Verification?

OTP Verification helps you:

- Reduce fake Cash on Delivery (COD) orders.
- Minimize Return-to-Origin (RTO) losses.
- Verify customer mobile numbers before checkout.
- Send OTP via **SMS** or **WhatsApp**.
- Provide a faster and more secure checkout experience.

---

## Step 1: OTP Verification

When a customer clicks **Checkout**, RabbitPay displays the OTP Verification screen before allowing the customer to continue.

The customer enters the OTP sent to their registered mobile number.

## Step 2: Receive the OTP

RabbitPay sends a One-Time Password (OTP) to the customer's registered mobile number through **SMS** or **WhatsApp**, depending on your configured notification channel.

<Frame>
  ![Whats App Image 2026 08 06 At 8 10 16 PM](/images/WhatsApp-Image-2026-08-06-at-8.10.16-PM.jpeg "Whats App Image 2026 08 06 At 8 10 16 PM")
</Frame>

---

## Step 3: Verify the Mobile Number

After entering the correct OTP, RabbitPay verifies the customer's mobile number and securely authenticates the order.

---

## Step 4: Continue to Checkout

Once the mobile number is successfully verified, the customer is redirected to the RabbitPay checkout to review their order and choose a payment method.

  ![Screenshot 2026 08 06 201222](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-06-201222.png "Screenshot 2026 08 06 201222")
</Frame>

---

## Checkout Experience

After successful OTP verification:

- Customers continue to checkout without interruption.
- The verified mobile number is linked to the order.
- Payment methods are displayed based on your RabbitPay configuration.
- Customers can securely complete their purchase.

---

## Result

Only customers who successfully verify their mobile number can proceed to the RabbitPay checkout.

OTP Verification helps reduce fake COD orders, improves order quality, and provides a secure and seamless checkout experience for both merchants and customers.
