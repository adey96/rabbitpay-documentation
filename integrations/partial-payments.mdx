# How to Set Up Partial Payment in RabbitPay

Collect an advance payment for Cash on Delivery (COD) orders to reduce Return-to-Origin (RTO) losses and improve order commitment. RabbitPay allows you to configure flexible partial payment rules based on percentage or fixed amount.

---

## Before You Begin

Make sure:

- RabbitPay is installed and connected to your Shopify store.
- You have access to the RabbitPay Dashboard.
- Your checkout is already configured with RabbitPay.

---

## Step 1: Open the RTO Suite

1. Log in to your RabbitPay Dashboard.
2. From the left sidebar, click **RTO Suite**.
3. You'll see the **Payment Rules** page where all your payment rules are listed.

> **Note:** Payment rules are evaluated from top to bottom. If multiple rules match an order, the first matching rule will be applied.

---

## Step 2: Create a New Payment Rule

Click the **Add payment rule** button in the top-right corner.

This opens the payment rule configuration page where you can define how much customers need to pay upfront.

# Configure the Payment Rule

Complete the following sections based on your business requirements.

---

## Rule Details

### Rule Name

Enter a meaningful name for your rule.

**Example**

- Part Payment
- Reserve Your Order
- 25% Advance
- Booking Amount

---

## Amount

Choose how the customer should pay upfront.

### Amount Type

You can choose between:

- **Percentage** – Collect a percentage of the order value.
- **Fixed Amount** – Collect a fixed amount regardless of cart value.

### Amount

Enter the value.

**Example**

| Amount Type | Value | Customer Pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Percentage | 25 | 25% of the order value |
| Fixed Amount | 199 | ₹199 upfront |

---

## Cart Range (Optional)

Limit the rule to specific cart values.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum Cart Total | Minimum order amount required |
| Maximum Cart Total | Maximum order amount eligible |

**Example**

| Minimum | Maximum |
| --- | --- |
| ₹500 | ₹10,000 |

The rule will only apply to orders within this range.

---

## Partial COD Fee (Optional)

Enable **Include COD fee on delivery** if you want to charge an additional COD fee when the customer pays the remaining amount upon delivery.

This is useful if you want to:

- Recover COD handling costs.
- Encourage prepaid payments.
- Reduce non-serious COD orders.

---

## Discount (Optional)

Reward customers for paying partially online.

### Discount Code

Enter the Shopify discount code.

Example:

```text
PREPAID10
```

### Discount Type

Choose one:

- Fixed Amount
- Percentage

### Discount Value

Enter the discount amount.

Examples:

- ₹50
- 10%

Customers who choose the partial payment option will receive this discount during checkout.

---

## Applies To

Choose where this payment rule should apply.

### All Products

Applies to every product in your store.

Recommended if you want partial payment on all COD orders.

---

### Only Selected

Apply the rule only to selected products.

Ideal for:

- High-value items
- Limited edition products
- Custom products

---

### All Except Selected

Apply the rule to every product except the products you exclude.

Useful if only a few products should remain eligible for normal COD.

---

## Zipcode Restrictions (Optional)

Restrict the payment rule based on delivery pincodes.

You can:

- Include selected pincodes
- Exclude selected pincodes

This is useful if certain locations have higher RTO rates.

---

## Customer Restrictions (Optional)

Choose whether the rule should apply to:

- New customers
- Returning customers
- All customers

---

## Save the Rule

After configuring all required fields:

1. Review your settings.
2. Click **Save Changes**.
3. Your payment rule will now appear in the Payment Rules list.

# How It Works at Checkout

When a customer reaches RabbitPay Checkout:

- They will see the **Part Payment** option.
- The checkout displays:
  - Amount payable now
  - Remaining COD amount
  - Any applicable discount
  - COD fee (if enabled)

Example:

- Order Total: ₹990
- Partial Payment: 25%
- Customer Pays Now: ₹247.50
- Remaining COD Amount: ₹742.50

# Example Configuration

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rule Name | Part Payment |
| Amount Type | Percentage |
| Amount | 25% |
| Cart Range | ₹500 – ₹10,000 |
| Discount | PREPAID10 |
| Discount Type | Fixed Amount |
| Discount Value | ₹50 |
| Applies To | All Products |
| COD Fee | Disabled |

# Result

Customers placing eligible COD orders will:

- Pay 25% upfront.
- Receive any configured discount.
- Pay the remaining amount upon delivery.
- Experience a smooth RabbitPay checkout while helping reduce RTO risk for your business.
