## Step 1: Open RTO Suite

1. Log in to your RabbitPay Dashboard.
2. Go to **RTO Suite**.
3. Click **Add payment rule**.
4. <Frame>
     ![Add Paymenr Rule](/images/add-paymenr-rule.png "Add Paymenr Rule")
   </Frame>

---

## Step 2: Configure the Rule

Fill in the following details:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rule Name | **Part Payment** _(Recommended)_ |
| Other Examples | Reserve Your Order,<br />Book Now,<br />25% Advance |
| Amount Type | Percentage or Fixed Amount |
| Amount | Enter the upfront payment amount to collect |

<Frame>
  ![Edit Payment Rule](/images/edit-payment-rule.png "Edit Payment Rule")
</Frame>

> **Tip:** Use a clear rule name that customers will see during checkout.

---

## Step 3: Configure Optional Settings

If required, configure:

- **Cart Range** – Apply the rule only for selected order values.
- **Partial COD Fee** – Charge a COD fee on the remaining amount.
- **Discount** – Apply a Shopify discount code.
- **Applies To** – All products, selected products, or all except selected.
- **Zipcode Restrictions** – Restrict the rule to specific zipcodes.

---

## Step 4: Save the Rule

1. Enable **Rule Enabled**.
2. Click **Save Changes**.

  ![Screenshot 2026 08 01 223304 2](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-01-223304-2.png "Screenshot 2026 08 01 223304 2")

## Checkout Preview

Customers will see the **Part Payment** option during checkout.

  ![Checkout](/images/rto-suite/partial-payment-checkout.png)

## Example Configuration

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rule Name | Part Payment |
| Amount Type | Percentage |
| Amount | 25% |
| Cart Range | ₹500 – ₹10,000 (Optional) |
| Applies To | All Products |

## Result

Your partial payment rule is now active and will be applied to eligible orders based on the conditions you've configured.
