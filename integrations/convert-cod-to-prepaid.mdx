# How to Convert COD Orders to Prepaid at Shopify Checkout

RabbitPay encourages customers to choose **Prepaid** instead of **Cash on Delivery (COD)** by displaying one-click online payment options and highlighting prepaid savings during checkout.

---

## Why Encourage Prepaid Payments?

Choosing prepaid payments helps your business:

- Reduce Return-to-Origin (RTO) losses.
- Minimize fake or non-serious COD orders.
- Receive payments before dispatching orders.
- Improve cash flow.
- Increase prepaid order conversions by offering discounts.

---

## How It Works

When a customer reaches the RabbitPay checkout:

- Customers immediately see **one-click UPI payment options**.
- Supported UPI apps such as **Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM** are displayed for faster payments.
- A prepaid savings banner is shown to encourage online payment.
- Customers can also choose Cards, Wallets, or Net Banking if preferred.

## One-Click UPI Checkout

Customers can complete their payment directly using their preferred UPI application without searching for payment methods.

RabbitPay automatically displays the supported UPI apps available on the customer's device, making the checkout experience faster and more convenient.

## Checkout Experience

During checkout, RabbitPay:

- Displays available prepaid payment methods.
- Highlights prepaid savings.
- Shows one-click UPI payment options.
- Allows customers to complete payment quickly using their preferred payment method.

---

## Result

Customers are encouraged to complete their purchase using **Prepaid** instead of **Cash on Delivery (COD)**.

By making prepaid payment more convenient and highlighting available savings, merchants can increase prepaid conversions, reduce COD orders, and lower Return-to-Origin (RTO) losses.
