---

Prepaid discounts encourage customers to pay online instead of choosing Cash on Delivery (COD). When a customer selects a prepaid payment method, RabbitPay automatically applies the discount you configure.

## Step 1: Open the RTO Suite

1. Log in to your RabbitPay Dashboard.
2. From the left sidebar, click **RTO Suite**.
3. Find the payment rule where you want to offer a prepaid discount.
4. Click the **Edit** (✏️) icon.

## Step 2: Create or Edit a Payment Rule

You can:

- **Edit an existing payment rule**, or
- **Create a new payment rule** by clicking **Add payment rule**.

Fill in the required details, such as:

- **Rule name**
- **Amount type**
- **Minimum cart value** (if required)
- **Maximum cart value** (if required)

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 02 202116](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-02-202116.png "Screenshot 2026 08 02 202116")
</Frame>

---

## Step 3: Add the Prepaid Discount

Scroll down to the **Discount** section.

Enter the following details:

- **Discount code** – Enter the discount code you want to show.
- **Discount type** – Choose **Fixed Amount** or **Percentage**.
- **Discount value** – Enter the discount amount or percentage.

### Example

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Discount Code | SAVE100 |
| Discount Type | Fixed Amount |
| Discount Value | ₹100 |

>   ![Screenshot 2026 08 02 204056](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-02-204056.png "Screenshot 2026 08 02 204056")

---

## Step 4: Save the Rule

After entering all the details, click **Save changes**.

RabbitPay will now apply the discount whenever a customer chooses a prepaid payment method.

---

## Step 5: Verify the Discount

Visit your store and proceed to checkout.

When you select a prepaid payment method, you should see:

- The prepaid discount displayed.
- The discounted prepaid amount.
- The normal Cash on Delivery amount.

## Result

Your prepaid discount is now active. Customers will automatically receive the configured discount whenever they choose a prepaid payment method during checkout.
