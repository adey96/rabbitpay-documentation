# How to Set Up COD Fees

RabbitPay allows you to add an additional **Cash on Delivery (COD) fee** for eligible orders. You can configure a fixed COD fee, apply it to specific products, restrict it by cart value or delivery zipcode, and control when the fee appears during checkout.

---

## Before You Begin

Make sure:

- RabbitPay is installed and connected to your Shopify store.
- You have access to the RabbitPay Dashboard.
- COD payment is enabled for your store.

---

## Step 1: Open the RTO Suite

1. Log in to your RabbitPay Dashboard.
2. Navigate to **RTO Suite**.
3. Click **Add Payment Rule**.

<Frame>
  ![Add Payment Rule](/images/add-payment-rule.png "Add Payment Rule")
</Frame>

---

## Step 2: Create a COD Fee Rule

Configure the following fields.

### Rule Name

Enter a descriptive name.

**Example**

- COD Fee
- COD Charge
- Cash on Delivery Fee

### Amount Type

Select **COD** from the dropdown.

### Amount

Enter the COD fee you want to charge.

**Example**

| Amount |
| --- |
| ₹49 |
| ₹59 |
| ₹99 |

> **Note**
>
> If the **Amount** field is left blank or set to **0**, no additional COD fee will be charged.

## Step 3: Configure Optional Settings

### Cart Range

Limit the COD fee to specific order values.

Example:

- Minimum Cart Total: ₹500
- Maximum Cart Total: ₹10,000

The COD fee will only apply to orders within this range.

---

### Discount (Optional)

You can optionally apply a Shopify discount code together with the COD rule if required.

---

### Applies To

Choose where the COD fee should apply.

- **All Products** – Apply the COD fee across your entire store.
- **Only Selected** – Apply the COD fee only to selected products.
- **All Except Selected** – Apply the COD fee to every product except the excluded products.

This gives you complete flexibility to charge COD fees only for specific products or collections.

---

### COD Availability

Enable **Show COD as disabled when blocked** if you want customers to see COD as unavailable instead of completely hiding the payment option.

---

### Zipcode Restrictions

Enable **Zipcode Restrictions** to control where the COD fee rule is applied.

You can:

- Restrict specific zipcodes.
- Restrict a range of zipcodes.
- Apply the rule only to selected delivery locations.

This is useful for high-RTO regions or locations with additional delivery charges.

---

### Rule Status

Enable **Rule Enabled** to activate the rule.

Click **Save Changes** once your configuration is complete.

## Customer Checkout Experience

When customers choose **Cash on Delivery** during RabbitPay Checkout, the configured COD fee is automatically added to the order total.

<Frame>
  ![Partial Payment Checkout](/images/partial-payment-checkout.png "Partial Payment Checkout")
</Frame>

Example:

| Order Total | ₹990 |
| --- | --- |
| COD Fee | ₹59 |
| Customer Pays | ₹1,049 |

## Example Configuration

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rule Name | COD Charge |
| Amount Type | COD |
| Amount | ₹59 |
| Cart Range | ₹500 – ₹10,000 (Optional) |
| Applies To | All Products |
| Zipcode Restrictions | Enabled (Optional) |
| Rule Status | Enabled |

---

## Result

Customers selecting **Cash on Delivery** during RabbitPay Checkout will automatically see the configured COD fee added to their payable amount. The rule can be limited by cart value, specific products, or selected zipcodes, giving you complete control over when COD fees are applied.
