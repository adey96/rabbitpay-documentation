RabbitPay is a certified Shopify app that installs in minutes and syncs orders, customers, and inventory bidirectionally.

## What gets installed

- A native **Shopify app** in your admin
- A **theme app extension** that renders the Buy Now button
- Webhook subscriptions for `orders/*`, `refunds/*`, and `customers/*`
- A private connection to the RabbitPay backend for order creation and updates

## Requirements

- Shopify plan: **Basic** or higher
- Any published theme running **Online Store 2.0**
- A RabbitPay account with **Shopify** enabled

## Install steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install from the Shopify App Store">
    Go to [apps.shopify.com/rabbitpay](https://apps.shopify.com/rabbitpay) and click **Add app**. Approve the requested scopes.

    | Scope | Purpose |
    | --- | --- |
    | `read_products` | Fetch product details for checkout |
    | `write_orders` | Create orders on successful checkout |
    | `read_customers` | Match returning shoppers |
    | `write_draft_orders` | Handle partial payments and COD |
    | `read_inventory` | Enforce stock before authorization |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your RabbitPay merchant account">
    On first launch, log in with your RabbitPay credentials. The app links your Shopify store to your RabbitPay merchant ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the theme app embed">
    From Shopify Admin → **Online Store → Themes → Customize**, open the **App embeds** panel and toggle **RabbitPay Buy Now** on. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure checkout settings">
    In the RabbitPay app → **Settings**, choose which SKUs, collections, or customer segments the Buy Now button appears for. Configure COD eligibility, partial payment thresholds, and shipping rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test in draft mode">
    Toggle **Test mode** in the app. Complete a test purchase using OTP `123456`. Verify the draft order appears in Shopify Admin → Orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Flip **Test mode** off. Buy Now goes live for all matching products immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Order sync

When a shopper completes checkout, RabbitPay creates the order on Shopify via the Admin API and applies the following mapping:

| RabbitPay field | Shopify field |
| --- | --- |
| `line_items[].sku` | `line_items[].sku` |
| `customer.phone` | `customer.phone` |
| `shipping_address` | `shipping_address` |
| `payment_method` | Order tag: `rabbitpay:prepaid`, `rabbitpay:cod`, `rabbitpay:partial` |
| `session.id` | Order note attribute: `rabbitpay_session_id` |

<Tip>
  Use the `rabbitpay_session_id` note attribute to correlate Shopify orders with RabbitPay sessions in your BI or warehouse.
</Tip>

## Webhooks from Shopify → RabbitPay

RabbitPay subscribes to these Shopify topics automatically:

- `orders/create`, `orders/updated`, `orders/cancelled`, `orders/fulfilled`
- `refunds/create`
- `customers/create`, `customers/update`

These keep RabbitPay's records in sync when merchants edit orders directly in Shopify Admin.

## Uninstalling

Removing the RabbitPay app from Shopify immediately disables the Buy Now button. Existing orders remain intact. RabbitPay retains order records for 90 days and then purges customer PII per our [Data Retention Policy](https://rabbitpay.ai/legal/data-retention).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Buy Now button not appearing">
    Confirm the app embed is enabled **and** the theme is published (not just saved). Check that the product isn't in an excluded collection under app settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Orders not showing in Shopify">
    Open the RabbitPay Dashboard → Sessions and inspect the session. Look for a `shopify.order_create_failed` error — usually caused by inventory or shipping zone misconfiguration.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Discounts not applying">
    RabbitPay respects Shopify automatic discounts and script-based discounts. Discount codes must be enabled per SKU in the RabbitPay app settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
