Use RabbitPay payment rules to disable Cash on Delivery (COD) for specific products while keeping COD available for the rest of your store.

This is useful when you want to restrict COD for products that are high-value, fragile, made-to-order, or have a higher risk of returns.

---

## Step 1: Open RTO Suite

1. Log in to your RabbitPay Dashboard.
2. Go to **RTO Suite**.
3. Click **Add payment rule**.

<Frame>
  ![Add Paymenr Rule 1](/images/add-paymenr-rule-1.png "Add Paymenr Rule 1")
</Frame>

---

## Step 2: Configure the COD Rule

Enter the following details (example) :

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rule Name | Cash on delivery |
| Amount Type | COD |
| Amount | Enter the COD fee, if required |

If you want to charge a COD fee for eligible products, enter the fee amount.

For example:

- Amount Type: **COD**
- Amount: **₹59**

If you do not want to charge an additional COD fee, you can leave the amount as required by your rule configuration.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 10 190951 1](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-10-190951-1.png "Screenshot 2026 08 10 190951 1")
</Frame>

---

## Step 3: Set the Rule to Exclude Selected Products

Under **Applies To**, select:

**All except selected**

This allows you to keep the COD rule active for your store while excluding specific products from COD.

<Frame>
  ![Disable Cod On Product 1](/images/disable-cod-on-product-1.png "Disable Cod On Product 1")
</Frame>

---

## Step 4: Select the Products

You can add the products you want to exclude from COD in two ways.

### Option 1: Enter Product IDs Manually

Enter the Shopify Product IDs in the **Product IDs** field.

For example:

```text
7234567890123, 7838549606607, 6543210987654
```

### Option 2: Select Products

Click **Select products** and choose the products you want to exclude from COD.

1. Search for a product by name.
2. Select the required products.
3. Click **Save**.

RabbitPay will add the selected products to the **Product IDs** field.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 10 191112 1](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-10-191112-1.png "Screenshot 2026 08 10 191112 1")
</Frame>

---

## Step 5: Save the Rule

Review your settings and click **Save changes**.

Make sure the rule is enabled before saving.

---

## Result

When a customer adds one of the excluded products to their cart, **Cash on Delivery will not be available at checkout**.

Other payment methods such as UPI, cards, wallets, and Net Banking will remain available.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 10 191221](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-10-191221.png "Screenshot 2026 08 10 191221")
</Frame>

For example, if you exclude a product from COD, the customer will see the available prepaid payment methods, but the **Cash on Delivery** option will not appear.

---

## Why Disable COD for Specific Products?

Restricting COD for selected products can help you:

- Reduce RTO on high-risk products.
- Avoid COD orders for expensive products.
- Protect fragile or made-to-order products.
- Encourage customers to use prepaid payment methods.
- Keep COD available for products where it works well.

---

## Example

Suppose you sell three products:

| Product | COD |
| --- | --- |
| Product A | Available |
| Product B | Available |
| Product C | Disabled |

If **Product C** is added to the excluded product list, customers will not see the COD option when purchasing that product.

---

## Result

Your selected products are now excluded from the COD rule, while COD remains available for the other eligible products in your store.

> **Note:** RabbitPay evaluates payment rules in order. Make sure your product-specific rule is placed correctly in the payment rule list so that it is not overridden by another broader rule.
