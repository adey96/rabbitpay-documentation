Use Zipcode Restrictions to apply or hide a payment rule for selected delivery zipcodes or zipcode ranges.

---

## Step 1: Open RTO Suite

1. Log in to your RabbitPay Dashboard.
2. Navigate to **RTO Suite**.
3. Click **Add payment rule** or edit an existing payment rule.

<Frame>
  ![Add Paymenr Rule 1](/images/add-paymenr-rule-1.png "Add Paymenr Rule 1")
</Frame>

---

## Step 2: Configure the Payment Rule

Configure the payment rule based on your requirement.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rule Name | Enter a customer-friendly rule name |
| Amount Type | Percentage, Fixed Amount, Full Payment, or COD |
| Amount | Configure according to your payment rule |

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 06 174902 1](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-06-174902-1.png "Screenshot 2026 08 06 174902 1")
</Frame>

> **Note:** In this guide, **Cash on Delivery (COD)** is used as an example. Zipcode Restrictions work with any payment rule.

---

## Step 3: Enable Zipcode Restrictions

Scroll down to the **Zipcode Restrictions** section.

1. Enable **Zipcode Restrictions**.
2. Enter the zipcodes where you want this payment rule to be restricted.

<Frame>
  ![Zipcode Restriction Annotation](/images/zipcode_restriction_annotation.png "Zipcode Restriction Annotation")
</Frame>

> **Tip:** You can highlight the entire **Zipcode Restrictions** section with a red border and use a red arrow pointing to **Enable Zipcode Restrictions**.

---

## Step 4: Add Zipcodes

You can enter individual zipcodes, zipcode ranges, or a combination of both.

| Type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Individual Zipcodes | `560001, 560009, 734004` |
| Zipcode Range | `734004-734008` |
| Mixed | `734004-734008, 560004, 560009` |

<Note>
  Separate multiple zipcodes using commas or new lines. Zipcode ranges are also supported.
</Note>

---

## Step 5: Save the Rule

1. Enable **Rule Enabled**.
2. Click **Save Changes**.

---

## Checkout Preview

Customers whose delivery zipcode matches the configured restrictions will not see the configured payment rule during checkout.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 06 174049](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-06-174049.png "Screenshot 2026 08 06 174049")
</Frame>

---

## Example Configuration

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rule Name | Cash on Delivery |
| Amount Type | COD |
| Zipcode Restrictions | Enabled |
| Restricted Zipcodes | 734004–734008, 560004, 560009 |
| Rule Status | Enabled |

---

## Result

The payment rule is now restricted to the configured zipcodes.

In this example, since the rule is configured for **Cash on Delivery (COD)**, customers whose delivery zipcode matches the configured restrictions will **not** see the **Cash on Delivery (COD)** payment option during checkout. Customers from all other eligible zipcodes will continue to see the COD payment option normally.
