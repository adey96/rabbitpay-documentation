## Collaborate Store with RabbitPay {#collaborate-store}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access your collaboration settings in Shopify" id="collaborate">
    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 08 01 180019 1](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-01-180019-1.png "Screenshot 2026 08 01 180019 1")
    </Frame>

    - Log in to your Shopify Admin account.
    - Click **Settings** from the bottom-left corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your collaboration code" id="collaboration-code">
    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 08 01 180219](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-01-180219.png "Screenshot 2026 08 01 180219")
    </Frame>

    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload your screenshot here */}

    - Go to **Users** → **Security**.
    - Scroll to **Collaborators**.
    - Copy the **Collaboration Code**.
    - Share the **Collaboration Code** together with your **Store URL** with the RabbitPay team.

    <Warning>
      Do not generate a new Collaboration Code until the RabbitPay integration has been completed.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the collaboration request" id="accept-request">
    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 08 01 190429](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-01-190429.png "Screenshot 2026 08 01 190429")
    </Frame>

    - You'll receive a collaboration request from the **RabbitPay Developers** team.
    - Review the request.
    - Click **Accept Request**.

    <Info>
      The collaborator access is used only to install and configure RabbitPay.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>
