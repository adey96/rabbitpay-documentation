Activate and configure **RabbitPay One Click Checkout** on your Shopify store to provide customers with a faster and simpler checkout experience.

Follow these steps to complete the setup and verify your checkout before going live.

### Prerequisites

Before starting the setup, make sure:

1. You have an active **Shopify store**.
2. You have access to your **Shopify Admin**.
3. RabbitPay is installed or ready to be installed on your store.
4. You have access to the **RabbitPay Dashboard**.

### Shopify Collaborator Access

RabbitPay may require collaborator access to your Shopify store for installation and configuration.

To share your collaborator access:

1. **Log in** to your Shopify Admin.
2. Navigate to **Settings \> Users and permissions**.
3. Scroll down to the **Collaborators** section.
4. Copy your **Collaborator request code**.
5. Share the code and your Shopify store URL with the RabbitPay team.

<Note>
  Never share your Shopify account password. The collaborator request code allows the RabbitPay team to request the required access to your store.
</Note>

### Installation

Once the required access is provided, the RabbitPay team will install and configure the RabbitPay app for your Shopify store.

After installation:

1. Log in to your **Shopify Admin**.
2. Go to **Settings \> Apps and sales channels**.
3. Confirm that **RabbitPay** is listed under your installed apps.
4. Open RabbitPay to access your RabbitPay Dashboard.

### Configure One Click Checkout

After RabbitPay is installed:

1. Open the **RabbitPay Dashboard**.
2. Go to the **Checkout** section.

1. Configure the checkout options according to your store requirements.
2. Save your settings.

### Configure Payment Methods

RabbitPay Checkout supports multiple payment options depending on your enabled payment integrations.

You can configure payment options such as:

- UPI
- Cards
- Net Banking
- Wallets
- Cash on Delivery
- Partial Payment
- Other enabled payment methods

Make sure your required payment integrations are configured before testing the checkout.

### Go Live

After completing your testing:

1. Review your RabbitPay Checkout settings.
2. Confirm that all required payment methods are enabled.
3. Place a final test order.
4. Verify the order in your Shopify Admin.
5. Your RabbitPay One Click Checkout is ready for customers.

<Note>
  If you make changes to your Shopify theme or checkout configuration after setup, test RabbitPay Checkout again to make sure it continues to work correctly.
</Note>
