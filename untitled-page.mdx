## Why Offer a Discount for Prepaid Orders?

Offering a discount for customers who pay online can help your store:

- **Increase prepaid orders** — Give customers a direct incentive to choose online payment instead of COD.
- **Reduce RTO risk** — Prepaid orders are less exposed to non-payment at the time of delivery.
- **Improve cash flow** — Receive the order payment upfront instead of collecting it at delivery.
- **Reduce COD costs** — Fewer COD orders can help reduce costs related to cash collection and handling.

## Step 1: Open Payment Rules

From your RabbitPay dashboard:

1. Go to **RTO Suite**.
2. Open **Payment Rules**.
3. Click **\+ Add payment rule**.
   <Frame>
     ![Add Paymenr Rule 1](/images/add-paymenr-rule-1.png "Add Paymenr Rule 1")
   </Frame>

## Step 2: Select Full Payment

In the payment rule settings:

1. Enter a **Rule name**, such as `Full Payment`.
2. Under **Amount type**, select **Full payment**.
3. Full payment means the customer pays **100% of the order amount online**.
4. If required, set a **minimum cart total** and **maximum cart total** to control which orders can use the rule.

For example, you can create a rule that applies only to orders between **₹500 and ₹10,000**.

## Step 3: Add the Discount

Under **Discount (Optional)**:

1. Enter your **Discount code**.
2. Select the **Discount type**.
3. Enter the **Discount value**.

<Frame>
  ![Discount Full Payment](/images/discount-full-payment.png "Discount Full Payment")
</Frame>

For example:

- **Discount code:** `FLAT100OFF`
- **Discount type:** Fixed amount or Percentage
- **Discount value:** ₹100 or 10%

The discount can then be offered to customers when they choose full prepaid payment.

> **Note:** The discount code and amount shown above are examples. You can configure your own discount

## tep 4: Choose Which Products the Rule Applies To

Under **Applies to**, choose where the payment rule should work.

You have three options:

- **All products** — Apply the rule to all products in the cart.
- **Only selected** — Apply the rule only to selected products.
- **All except selected** — Apply the rule to all products except the products you exclude.

This gives you control over which products can receive the prepaid discount.

## Step 5: Save the Rule

After configuring the payment type, cart range, discount and product conditions:

1. Review your settings.
2. Make sure **Rule enabled** is turned on.
3. Click **Save changes**.

Your full prepaid discount rule is now active.

## What Customers See at Checkout

When a customer reaches RabbitPay Checkout, the available payment methods are displayed.

The prepaid payment option can show the configured saving, making it clear that the customer can receive a discount by choosing online payment.

The customer can then select an online payment method and complete **100% of the order amount upfront**.

## How RabbitPay Helps

RabbitPay connects your payment rules directly with the checkout experience.

Instead of offering a discount to every customer, you can use payment rules to encourage customers to choose the payment method that is better for your business.

The flow is simple:

**Customer chooses full prepaid payment → Discount is offered → Customer pays online → Order is paid upfront**

This gives customers an immediate benefit while helping merchants move more orders from COD to prepaid.

## Benefits of Using RabbitPay for Prepaid Discounts

### Lower RTO

Encourage prepaid orders and reduce the risk of orders being returned because of non-payment.

### Upfront Payment

Receive payment before the order is shipped instead of waiting for payment on delivery.

### Flexible Rules

Apply the prepaid offer based on cart value or specific products.

### Better Customer Incentive

Give customers an immediate discount for choosing online payment.

This helps your Shopify store encourage more online payments while reducing dependence on COD.

**Tip:** Test the checkout after saving the rule to make sure the payment option and discount are displayed correctly for eligible orders.
